Corporate & Transactional

We serve as trusted counsel to businesses at every stage — from formation and contracts to acquisitions and ongoing general-counsel support.

Our corporate attorneys advise entrepreneurs, business owners, and cross-border investors on entity formation, governance, contracts, and the transactions that drive their businesses forward. We structure and close acquisitions, negotiate key agreements, and help clients manage risk as they grow.

We also serve as outside general counsel to companies — including foreign businesses that need a U.S. presence or affiliate — providing the day-to-day guidance that keeps operations compliant and deals on track.

With deep experience in cross-border matters, we help international clients navigate the U.S. legal landscape and bridge cultural and legal gaps. We serve clients in both English and Mandarin.
