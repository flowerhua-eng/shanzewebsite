Immigration

We help individuals, founders, and companies navigate U.S. immigration — from family- and employment-based petitions to complex business visas and appeals.

Our immigration practice advises on, prepares, and files a wide range of immigrant and non-immigrant petitions, including I-130, I-140 (EB-1/NIW), PERM, EB-4, I-485, L-1, H-1B, and O-1. We help clients respond to requests for evidence, appeal unfavorable decisions, and resolve complex or time-sensitive issues.

We work closely with business clients on employment-based sponsorship and with individuals and families on their path to residency, always attentive to the deadlines and documentation that determine outcomes.

Immigration frequently intersects with business and investment matters, and we represent clients in immigration-related investment disputes as well. We serve clients in both English and Mandarin.

Capabilities

  • Family-Based Petitions (I-130)
  • Employment-Based Petitions (I-140)
  • EB-1 / NIW
  • PERM Labor Certification
  • L-1, H-1B & O-1 Visas
  • Adjustment of Status (I-485)
  • Requests for Evidence & Appeals
  • Immigration-Related Investment Disputes
