Estate Planning & Probate

We help individuals and families plan for the future and administer estates with care — from wills and trusts to guidance through probate.

Our attorneys prepare wills, trusts, and comprehensive estate plans tailored to each client’s family, assets, and goals. We help clients structure the orderly transfer of wealth, plan for incapacity, and minimize uncertainty for the people they care about.

When the time comes, we guide personal representatives and families through probate and the administration of estates, handling the filings, notices, and disputes that can arise along the way.

For our cross-border clients, we coordinate planning that accounts for assets and family in more than one country. We serve clients in both English and Mandarin.
