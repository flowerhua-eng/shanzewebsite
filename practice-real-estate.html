Real Estate

We advise buyers, sellers, landlords, tenants, and investors on the acquisition, leasing, financing, and disposition of commercial and residential property.

Our attorneys handle the full lifecycle of real estate transactions — from letter of intent and due diligence through purchase and sale agreements, financing documents, and closing — for individual investors, developers, and businesses acquiring or disposing of property in Washington and beyond.

We regularly draft and negotiate commercial leases for landlords and tenants, and we represent clients in commercial tenancy disputes, including unlawful detainer actions and lease enforcement. We also advise on entity structuring for real estate holdings, joint ventures between investors, and the real estate components of larger corporate transactions.

Because so much of our real estate work involves cross-border investors and entrepreneurs, we help clients navigate financing, title, and regulatory considerations that are unique to foreign buyers and owners of U.S. property. We serve clients in both English and Mandarin.
